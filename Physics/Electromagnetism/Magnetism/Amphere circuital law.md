$\displaystyle \oint{B \cdot dl} = \mu_0I = \mu_0 (I_c + I_d) = \mu_0 I_c + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$
- $I$: [[Displacement current]]
- $I_c$: Conduction current
- $I_d$: Total current
$$
\begin{align}
B = \mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{d}{dl}\frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}\\
\oint \vec E \cdot \vec dl = -\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{d}{dl}\frac{d^2\Phi_E}{dt^2}\\
\vec E = -\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{d^2}{dl^2}\frac{d^2E\cdot A}{dt^2}\\
\end{align}
