### In Series Circuit
- $V = V_1 + V_2$ 
- $I = I_1 = I_2$ ($I$ is constant)
$$
\displaystyle
\begin{align}
V_1 = I_1R_1\\
V_2 = I_2R_2\\

\implies V_1 = IR_1\\
\implies V_2 = IR_2\\
\implies V = V_1 + V_2 = IR_1 + IR_2 \\
\implies V = I(R_1 + R_2)\\
\implies R_{eq} = (R_1 + R_2)\\
\implies R_{eq} = \sum{R_i};\ \forall R_i\ in\ series\\
\end{align}
$$
### For Any $V_n$
$$
\displaystyle
\begin{align}
V_n = IR_n\\
\implies V_n = \frac{V}{R_{eq}}R_n = V\frac{R_n}{R_{eq}}\\
\end{align}
$$
