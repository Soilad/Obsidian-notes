## Linearity
> Must satisfy Homogeneity & Superposition
- #### Homogeneity (Scaling)
	- $f(x) = y$
	- If $f$ satisfies homogeneity $\implies f(kx) = ky$
- #### Superposition (Additivity)
	- $f(x_1) = y_1$
	- $f(x_1) = y_2$
	- If $f$ satisfies superposition $\implies f(x_1 + x_2) = y_1 + y_2$
- #### Examples
	- Resistor
	- Capacitor
	- Inductor
## Non-Linearity
> Doesn't satisfy Homogeneity or Superposition
- #### Examples
	- Diodes
	- Thermistor
	- Transistor
