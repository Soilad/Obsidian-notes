## Active ($\forall P, sign(P) \neq constant$)
- Able to supply energy
- ### Examples
	- Voltage sources
	- Current sources
	- Batteries
	- Generators
## Passive ($\forall P, sign(P) = constant$)
- Dissipates/Stores energy
- ### Examples
	- Resistor (Dissipates)
	- Capacitor (Stores)
	- Inductor (Stores)
