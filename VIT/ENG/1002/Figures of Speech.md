# Figure of speech
Deliberate use of words in a **non-literal or distinctive way** to make communication vivid, expressive, memorable or impactful. Not just poetry — ads, speeches, journalism, social media.

| Device | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Simile** | Comparison **using** *like* / *as* | "He is as brave as a lion" |
| **Metaphor** | One thing **described as** another — implied, no *like/as* | "All the world is a stage" |
| **Personification** | Human qualities given to non-human things | "The wind whispered through the trees" |
| **Hyperbole** | Deliberate exaggeration | "I've told you a million times" |
| **Pun** | Word with two meanings / sound-alikes, for humour | "It was two-tired" (two tyres / too tired) |
| **Irony** | Gap between appearance and reality | see below |
| **Oxymoron** | Two contradictory words together | *deafening silence* |
| **Anaphora** | Same phrase repeated at the **beginning** of successive clauses | "We shall fight on the beaches…" |
| **Epistrophe** | Same phrase repeated at the **end** of successive clauses | "…of the people, by the people, for the people" |
| **Alliteration** | Same consonant sound repeated | *Peter Piper picked…* |

> **Simile vs Metaphor** is the single most-tested pair. *Like/as* present → simile. Absent → metaphor.
> **Anaphora vs Epistrophe** — Anaphora = **A**t the start.

# Irony — two types
| Type | Gap between | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verbal Irony** | What is **said** vs what is **meant** | Getting an F and saying "Wow, I did a really good job!" |
| **Situational Irony** | What is **expected** vs what **happens** | A fire station catches fire |

More verbal: *"What a wonderful way to start the day!"* — after the bus arrives an hour late.
More situational: a swimming instructor afraid of water · a cybersecurity expert gets hacked · the "unsinkable" Titanic sank on its first voyage.

# Personification examples
- "Time waits for no one" — time as a person who can wait
- "Because I could not stop for Death – He kindly stopped for me" (Dickinson)
- "Opportunity knocked at my door"
- "The city never sleeps"

# Anaphora examples
- "I am strong; I am capable; I am worthy."
- "We demand to be heard. We demand action. We demand justice."
- Churchill: "**We shall fight** in France, **we shall fight** on the seas and oceans… we shall never surrender."
