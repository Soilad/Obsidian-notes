# Direct imperatives
Begin with the **base form of a verb**, omit the subject *you*. The single most important language feature of a [[User Manual]].

- Press the Power button.
- Connect the cable to the socket.
- Turn off the device before cleaning.
- Do not immerse the device in water.

**Why:** clear and direct · tells users exactly what to do · short · action-oriented · correct order · less ambiguity · **improves safety** by clearly stating what not to do.

# The three things to strip out
| Strip | ❌ | ✅ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Passive voice** | The battery should be inserted. | **Insert** the battery. |
| **Politeness markers** (*please, kindly, you are requested to*) | Please press the Power button. | **Press** the Power button. |
| **Modal verbs** (*should, could, might, must*) | You should insert the battery. | **Insert** the battery. |

Politeness markers make instructions lengthy, reduce directness, create a conversational rather than instructional tone.
Modal verbs make instructions less direct and create uncertainty about whether an action is compulsory or optional.

> [!warning] Exception — modals ARE correct for safety
> Warnings, recommendations and possibilities keep their modals:
> - **Must:** You must disconnect the power supply before servicing the appliance.
> - **May:** The device may become hot during operation.
> - **Should:** You should clean the filter regularly.

# Rewrite drill
| ❌ Given | ✅ Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Please press the Power button to switch on the device. | Press the Power button to switch on the device. |
| The power cable must be disconnected before cleaning. | Disconnect the power cable before cleaning the device. |
| You are requested to keep the device away from water. | Keep the device away from water. |
| The water tank should be filled up to the maximum level. | Fill the water tank up to the maximum level. |
| Kindly remove the plug from the socket before maintenance. | Remove the plug from the socket before maintenance. |
| You should not operate the machine with wet hands. | Do not operate the machine with wet hands. |
| The device should be stored in a cool and dry place. | Store the device in a cool and dry place. |

> Pattern: find the object → find the verb → put the verb first in base form → delete everything else.

# Active voice — why
- Clearly identifies the action the user must perform
- Avoids unnecessary words
- Identifies **who is responsible** (the user — without repeating *you*)
- Short active sentences are faster to follow
- Reduces ambiguity about action and object

# Sequence markers
| Stage | Markers |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginning** | First, Initially, To begin with, To start, Begin by |
| **Middle** | Next, Then, After that, Subsequently, Following this, Afterwards |
| **Ending** | Finally, Lastly, Once completed |

**Examples**
- First, insert the battery. Next, close the battery compartment.
- Initially, fill the tank with water. After that, close the lid.
- To start, plug in the charger. Subsequently, press the Power button.
- Start by removing the filter. Finally, clean it with a soft brush.

> [!tip] Reordering questions
> Given jumbled steps: work out the **real-world dependency** first (you can't close the compartment before inserting the battery), then attach markers. The marker must match the position — *Finally* can only be last.
