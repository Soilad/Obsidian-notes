# Résumé vs CV
| Feature | **CV** | **Résumé** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Length** | Longer — several pages | One to two pages |
| **Purpose** | Academic or research positions | Most job applications, business and industry |
| **Content** | Detailed: academic background, publications, research, teaching, memberships | Most relevant skills, experience, accomplishments |
| **Focus** | Academic achievements and qualifications | Professional experience and skills |

- **Résumé** — brief, **job-specific**, customised per application. Highlights only what matches the job.
- **CV** — comprehensive, complete career record. *Curriculum vitae* = "course of life".

# Types of résumé
| Type | Orders by | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chronological** | Work and education in **reverse chronological** order | Showing career progression, stability, growth |
| **Functional** | **Skills and competencies**, not work history | **Freshers**, students with limited experience, career changers |
| **Combination (Hybrid)** | Key skills at top **+** detailed work experience in reverse chronological order | Those with both skills and solid history |
| **Non-traditional** | Visual — graphics, charts, timelines, infographics; links to portfolios/GitHub; video résumés | Creative, tech, digital fields |

> Functional = *what you can do*, rather than *when or where* you gained it. That's the standard exam distinction, and the right answer for a first-year student.

# Résumé sections
1. **Personal details** — name, address, phone, email, LinkedIn/GitHub
2. **Career objective**
3. **Educational qualification** — table: Course · Institution · Year · CGPA/%
4. **Work experience**
5. **Internship / training**
6. **Personal details** — DOB, languages known, permanent address
7. **Declaration** + Place/Date

> **Declaration:** *I hereby declare that the above furnished particulars are true to the best of my knowledge and belief.*

# CV sections (fuller)
1. Personal information
2. Career objective
3. Educational qualifications
4. **Technical skills** — programming languages, tools, OS, networking, other
5. **Projects** — title, description, technologies used
6. Internships / industrial training
7. **Certifications**
8. Achievements / awards
9. Co-curricular / extracurricular
10. Personal skills — problem-solving, analytical thinking, teamwork, adaptability
11. **References** — name, designation, institution, email

# Career objective frame
> To secure a challenging position in **[field]** where I can apply my technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, and passion for **[domain]** while gaining professional growth.

> Pairs with a **cover letter** — see [[Letter Writing]] for format.
