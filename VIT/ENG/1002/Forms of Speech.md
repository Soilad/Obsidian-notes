# Four forms
| Form | Direction | Primary purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plain Speech** | One-way | Inform clearly |
| **Conversation** | Two-way | Exchange, interact |
| **Pitch** | One-way, targeted | Persuade → get a decision |
| **Dispute / Duel** | Opposed | Argue, defend, challenge |

# Plain Speech
Clear, direct, straightforward. Purpose is to **communicate the message**, not impress, entertain or persuade.
Used to: give information · explain · give instructions · describe · state facts · report · clarify.

# Conversation
Central feature is **exchange** — speaking, listening, responding. Two-way.
**Professional contexts:** meetings, interviews, discussions, telephone calls, team/client interactions, problem-solving, negotiations.

## Don'ts
1. **Interrupting**
2. **Not listening** — planning your reply instead of listening
3. **Dominating** — talking without giving others a turn
4. **Irrelevant responses** — changing topic without acknowledging
5. **Inappropriate language** — too casual for a formal situation
6. **Misunderstanding** — not asking for clarification

# Pitch
Concise + persuasive. Presents an idea/product/proposal to gain **interest, approval, support, or action**. Not a description — a *strategic* presentation with a **desired outcome**.

## Anatomy of a good pitch
1. Identify a **problem/need**
2. Propose a **solution**
3. Explain **benefits**
4. Provide **supporting evidence**
5. End with a **call to action**

## Types
| Type | Who → whom | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales** | Salesperson → customer | Purchase |
| **Business / Start-up** | Entrepreneur → investor | Investment or partnership |
| **Project** | Student/professional → panel | Approval or support |
| **Job / Self-pitch** | Candidate → employer | Get hired |

# Dispute / Duel
Verbal contest of ideas. Two or more speakers present **opposing viewpoints**, defend positions, challenge others' arguments.
Uses arguments and evidence. → see [[Modern Forms of Oration]] for debate rules and [[Logical Fallacies]] for what breaks an argument.
