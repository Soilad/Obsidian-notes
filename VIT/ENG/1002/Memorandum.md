# Memorandum (Memo)
Formal written message to communicate information, instructions, announcements, reminders, requests or decisions **within an organisation**.

> **Internal** communication — among people in the same organisation. Clearer and more efficient than a full formal letter.

# Purposes
| Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **To inform** | Revised timetable |
| **To announce** | A technical workshop |
| **To instruct** | Switch off lab equipment after use |
| **To remind** | Submit assignments |
| **To request action** | Submit question papers |
| **To explain** | Revised leave procedure |
| **To record decisions** | Decisions of a departmental review |

# Characteristics
| Quality | Bad → Good |
| --- | --- |
| **Clarity** | "The reports should be submitted soon." → "All students must submit their project reports **by 4:00 p.m. on 10 November 2026**." |
| **Conciseness** | "I am writing this memorandum in order to inform all the students that they are required to submit their assignments at the earliest possible time." → "All students are requested to submit their assignments **by Friday**." |
| **Accuracy** | All dates, times, venues, names must be correct |
| **Professional tone** | "Hey everyone, send your reports ASAP!" → "All concerned students are requested to submit their reports by the specified deadline." |
| **Logical organisation** | Purpose → details → required action |
| **Completeness** | A meeting memo needs date, time, venue, purpose, preparation |

# ⭐ Format
```
MEMORANDUM

To:      All Faculty Members
From:    Head, Department of English
Date:    28 October 2026
Subject: Revised Laboratory Timings

[Opening  — state the purpose]
[Details  — dates, times, venues, reasons, instructions]
[Action   — what recipients must do, by when, whom to contact]
```

> **Subject line:** weak → `Important Information`. Better → `Revised Laboratory Timings`.

# Model answer
> **MEMORANDUM**
> **To:** All Second-Year Engineering Students
> **From:** Head, Department of English
> **Date:** 28 October 2026
> **Subject:** Communication Skills Workshop
>
> All Second-Year Engineering Students are hereby informed that a Communication Skills Workshop will be conducted on 5 November 2026 from 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. in Seminar Hall 2.
>
> The workshop will focus on presentation skills, professional communication, and group discussions. All students are required to attend the session.
>
> Students must report to the venue by 9:45 a.m. and bring their university ID cards.
>
> For further information, please contact the Department of English.

# Reusable sentence frames
- Opening → *All employees are hereby informed that…*
- Effective date → *…will come into effect on [date].*
- Purpose → *The policy aims to…*
- Instruction → *With effect from the specified date, all employees must…*
- Responsibility → *Department heads are required to… They must also monitor compliance.*
- Problems → *Any technical problems must be reported promptly to the [X] department.*
- Cooperation → *All employees are requested to cooperate with the implementation of this policy.*
- Close → *For further clarification, employees may contact the [role].*

> [!warning] PYQ pattern
> *"Write a memorandum to your company staff"* — **10 marks**, usually **150–200 words**, with 3 bullet points of content you must include.
>
> Scoring: correct **To/From/Date/Subject** block · a **specific** subject line · the three-part body · professional tone. The header block alone is easy marks — never skip it.

> Memo = internal. [[Business Letter Types|Letter]] = external.
