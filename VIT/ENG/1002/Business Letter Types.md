# Business letter
Formal written communication between individuals, companies, customers, suppliers and organisations for professional or commercial purposes. Used to request information, report problems, place orders, respond to enquiries.

# The 6 C's — characteristics of a good business letter
| Quality | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Clarity** | Communicate the purpose directly |
| **Conciseness** | Only relevant information |
| **Courtesy** | Polite, professional language |
| **Correctness** | Grammatically and factually accurate |
| **Completeness** | All information the recipient needs to act |
| **Professional tone** | No slang, emotional language, informal expressions |

---
# Enquiry letter
Written to **obtain information** about a product, service, course, price, availability or business opportunity — before deciding or ordering.

**Examples:** price and specifications of a laptop · availability of books from a supplier · internship programme details · course fees and duration · hotel room availability · quotation for office equipment.

## Remember
- **State the purpose clearly** — what information you need
- **Ask specific questions** — name the product/service/programme
- **Request relevant details** — price, specifications, availability, delivery, warranty, payment terms
- **Use polite expressions**
- **Keep it concise**
- **End appropriately** — request a response and thank them

## Phrase bank
> I am writing to enquire about… · I would like to obtain information regarding… · Could you please provide details about…? · Kindly share your latest price list. · I would appreciate it if you could send me… · I look forward to receiving your response.

## Avoid
Vague questions ("Tell me about your products") · unrelated requests · informal expressions ("Hey", "Let me know ASAP") · demands instead of polite requests · long paragraphs with repeated questions.

# Complaint letter
Reports a **problem, defect, unsatisfactory service, delay, or failure** to meet agreed conditions, and requests a solution.

**Examples:** defective laptop · delayed delivery · damaged goods · poor internet service · incorrect bill · unsatisfactory service.

## Remember
- **Identify the problem clearly**
- **Provide relevant details** — order number, invoice number, purchase date, delivery date
- **Describe factually** — no exaggeration
- **Mention previous communication** — if you already contacted customer service, say when and how
- **Request a specific solution** — replacement, repair, refund, correction
- **Set a reasonable expectation** — a response within a reasonable period
- **Professional tone** — firm but courteous

## Phrase bank
> I am writing to bring to your attention… · I wish to report a problem with… · The product was delivered on… · Unfortunately, the item was found to be… · I request that you arrange for… · I would appreciate a prompt resolution. · Kindly look into this matter.

## Avoid
Rude or threatening language · unsupported accusations · exaggeration · irrelevant personal details · complaining without identifying the product · **failing to state what action you expect**.

# Order letter
Formal letter to **purchase or request supply** of specific goods. Communicates exactly what the buyer wants with details to process the order accurately.

**Examples:** computers for a college lab · books from a publisher · office furniture · lab equipment · stationery · electronic components.

## Remember
- **Identify products** — exact names and models
- **Specify quantities**
- **Include prices** where known / refer to an approved quotation
- **Specify delivery details** — address and required date
- **Mention payment terms**
- **Refer to the quotation** — reference number and date
- **Request confirmation**
- **Check all details**

## Phrase bank
> We would like to place an order for… · Please supply the following items… · As per your quotation dated… · The required quantity is… · Kindly deliver the items to… · Please confirm receipt of this order. · We look forward to timely delivery.

## Avoid
Products without models/specifications · omitting quantities · incomplete delivery address · unclear delivery or payment conditions · vague expressions ("Send us some computers").

# Response letter
Written to reply to an enquiry, complaint or order — acknowledging it and stating the action taken.

> [!warning] PYQ pattern
> *"As the Purchase Officer of Creative Solutions, write a formal letter to Satya Technologies complaining about the delay in delivery of their goods and poor quality of the same."* — **10 marks**
>
> Note the **role** given ("As the Purchase Officer"). Write from that role, and give yourself an invented order number and date — "relevant details" is an explicit marking criterion.
