# Hook – Line – Sinker
Metaphor from fishing: hook catches, line connects, sinker pulls it down. Idiom "fell for it hook, line and sinker" = completely convinced.

| Part | Role | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hook** | Grab attention | Opening |
| **Line** | The actual content — arguments, evidence, examples | Body (bulk) |
| **Sinker** | Leave lasting impact, call to action | Conclusion |

# Hook — 5 types
1. Ask a question
2. Make a surprising statement
3. Tell a short story
4. Use a quotation
5. Ask the audience to imagine

# Line
The **substance**. Main ideas, arguments, information, examples, evidence.
> Climate speech: scientific facts, examples of damage, possible solutions.

# Sinker — 3 ways to end
1. End with a **strong statement**
2. End with a **call to action**
3. End with a **thought-provoking question**

> [!tip] Exam use
> If asked to "write a speech on X", just lay it out as hook → line → sinker and label nothing. The structure does the marking for you.
