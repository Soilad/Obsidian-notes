# Four purposes
| Purpose | Aim | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inform** | Provide information | Explaining how AI works |
| **Persuade** | Convince audience to accept an idea | Convincing students to cut plastic use |
| **Entertain** | Make audience enjoy themselves | Telling a funny story |
| **Motivate** | Encourage people to **act** | Encouraging students before exams |

> Persuade = change their *mind*. Motivate = change their *behaviour*. That's the pair people confuse.

# Elements of effective oratory
1. **Content** — what you say
2. **Organisation** — how you arrange ideas
3. **Language** — words and sentences
4. **Delivery** — how you use your voice
5. **Body language**
6. **Audience connection**

# Why good speakers sound bad
Knowledge ≠ delivery. Common failures:
- Nervousness
- Poor organisation
- Unclear pronunciation
- Speaking too fast
- No eye contact
- Fillers — *uh, um, actually, like*

> [[Hook Line Sinker]] is the structure that fixes "poor organisation"
