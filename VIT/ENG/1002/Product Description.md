# Product description
Writing that explains **what a product is, what it offers, how it works, and why it's useful**. Not merely describing — helping the reader **recognise its value**. Engaging, informative, customer-focused.

# Elements (in order)
1. **Product name** — clearly identify it
2. **Introduction** — what it is and what it's designed for
3. **Physical description** — size, shape, colour, material, weight, design
4. **Key features** — the most important characteristics
5. **Functions and uses** — what it does, where/how it's used
6. **Benefits** — the practical value
7. **Specifications** — measurable information
8. **Closing statement** — summarise its suitability

# Worked example — *UrbanGo* Smart Backpack
> The **UrbanGo Smart Backpack** is a practical and stylish backpack designed for students, professionals, and everyday travellers. It features a sleek grey design made from **water-resistant polyester**, offering durability and protection for personal belongings. With a capacity of **25 litres**, the backpack has three compartments, including a **padded laptop compartment** that accommodates laptops up to **15.6 inches**. It also includes an **external USB charging port**, a side pocket for a water bottle, and durable zippers. Weighing only **850 grams**, it is lightweight and comfortable to carry. Measuring **45 × 30 × 18 cm**, it provides sufficient space without being bulky. With its organised storage, laptop protection, water-resistant material and convenient charging facility, the UrbanGo Smart Backpack is well suited for everyday academic, professional and travel needs.

> [!tip] Two registers
> The example above is the **neutral/technical** register. A **marketing** version of the same product opens with a benefit ("Carry everything you need with confidence…"), addresses the reader as *you*, and bolds the features. Match whichever the question asks for.

> Features are what it *has*. Benefits are what that **does for the reader**. Always pair them: *"850 grams — comfortable to carry throughout a busy day."*
