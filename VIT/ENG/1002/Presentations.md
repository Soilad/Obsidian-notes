# Presentation
Planned, structured communication to an audience. Unlike conversation it has a **specific purpose, a defined audience, and an organised structure**.

# What a good presentation does
| Goal | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **UNDERSTAND** | What are you saying? |
| **CONNECT** | Why does it matter? |
| **REMEMBER** | What should they take away? |
| **RESPOND** | What should they think, feel, or do? |

**Presentation skills** = the abilities to plan, organise, deliver and communicate a message effectively to an audience.
