```_graph
f(x) = x^2
```

### Concave Down
```_graph
f(x) = -x^2
```
### For $f(x)$ 
- **Points Of Inflection**: $f''(x) = 0$
- **Concave Down**: $f'(x)\uparrow$ or $f''(x) > 0$
- **Concave Down**: $f'(x)\downarrow$ or $f''(x) < 0$
```_system
y = x - (x^3)/(3!)
y = - (x^2)/(2)
y = - (x)
```
> Inflection points: $0$
> Concave Up: $(-\infty, 0)$
> Concave Down: $(0, \infty)$
