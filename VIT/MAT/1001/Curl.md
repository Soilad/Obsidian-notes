# Meaning:
- Basically how much a field's vectors go in loops
- **Synonyms for $\vec\nabla\times \vec f = 0$**:
	- Conservative
	- Irrotational 
# Formula: $\vec\nabla\times \vec f$
$$
\begin{align}
\vec\nabla\times \vec f = 0\\
\implies \frac{\partial}{\partial y}f\hat i = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}f\hat j\\
\implies \frac{\partial}{\partial z}f\hat i = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}f\hat k\\
\implies \frac{\partial}{\partial z}f\hat j = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}f\hat k
\end{align}
$$
