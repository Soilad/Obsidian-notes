# Meaning:
- the closed [[Line Integrals]] on a function = enclosed [[Surface Integral]] of [[Curl]]
# Formula: $\oint_{C} \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint (\nabla \times \vec{F}) dA$
$$
\begin{align}
\oint_{C} \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint (\nabla \times \vec{F}) dA\\
\implies \oint Pdx+Qdy = \iint (Q_{x}-P_{y})dA
\end{align}
$$
# Tips
- don't take $\hat{i}$ or $\hat{j}$ when computing curl
- if they ask for verification just compute both $\oint Pdx+Qdy, \iint (Q_{x}-P_{y})dA$
- the surface integral's components are ordered by the differentiating variable
