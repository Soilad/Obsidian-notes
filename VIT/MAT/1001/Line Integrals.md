# Meaning:
- Integral evaluated around a curve
# Formula: $\int \vec{F}\cdot \vec{r}$
$$
\begin{align}
\vec{F} = F_{x}\hat i+{F}_{y}\hat j+{F}_{z}\hat{k}\\
\vec{r} = r_{x}\hat i+{r}_{y}\hat j+{r}_{z}\hat{k}\\
\end{align}
$$
# Tips
- make $\vec{F}$ and $\vec{r}$ be in terms of one variable and integrate w.r.t that (direct integration)
- substitute $x=r_{x},y=r_{y},z=r_{z}$ in $\vec{F}$
- make sure u substitute it into 1 term EVERYWHERE
- switch the limits if u are going backwards
- compute the fucking $dx$ and $dy$ w.r.t to one variable
