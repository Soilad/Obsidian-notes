## Ke vibecoding?
> "computer coding that is done with the help of artificial intelligence, using natural language to give instructions" -- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/vibe-coding)

- In order to show the negatives of usage of LLMs in programming, this subtopic will be mainly referencing [this study](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3632620.3671116)
- The study aims to show the positives and negatives of AI on novice programmers
- In the study, most of the participants were designated to solve various programming problems with the assistant of ChatGPT and Github Copilot

> **Github Copilot**: LLM that provides autocomplete suggestions for programming
> **ChatGPT**: General purpose Large Language Model available online
## Participants who suffered (10)
- Most of the participants got distracted easily by Copilot's incorrect suggesions and had wrong approaches to the problems which were implemented by ChatGPT
- ChatGPT used certain concepts that the participants were not aware of (do while, std library)
- Students who used ChatGPT to give hints for the solution copied the generated code instead of reading the advice
- This lead to students
	- skipping several planning stages leading them to be clueless for implementation
	- having a false sense of
		- their own programming capabilities
		- acomplishment
		- learning how to solve the given problems
## Participants who suffered less (11)
- Most of the participants rarely accepted Copilot's suggestions
- little to no use of ChatGPT

> "Our findings suggest that students who are already poised to succeed can leverage GenAI to accelerate, while struggling students may be hindered by using GenAI, leaving them with an illusion of competence" -- The Study

## What this means for vibecoding
- Extending these conclusions to the practice of vibecoding, we can estimate that the negative effects of these LLM tools would be further exacerbated for novice programmers
## How this is is unethical
- **Virtue ethics**: Prevents you from being your best self. Therefore unethical.
- **Deontology**: Depends on whether or not vibecoding is an absolute moral duty.
- **Utilitarianism**: Does not seem to give best possible outcome for large amounts of people. Therefore unethical
