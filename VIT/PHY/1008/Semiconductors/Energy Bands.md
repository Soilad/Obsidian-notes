> Energy levels of many atoms merge into bands in solids.
## Band formation
> the closer the atoms are causes the energy levels to split between them
- 2 atoms: levels split into 2.
- 5 atoms: levels split into 5.
- Large number of atoms: levels spread into continuous bands.
- Equilibrium spacing $r_0$ sets band width.

## Bands
- **Valence band (VB)**: highest band, fully filled at 0 K.
- **Conduction band (CB)**: free electrons can move through material.
- **Forbidden energy gap ($E_g$)**: between VB and CB.

## Classification
| | Conductor | Semiconductor | Insulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| $E_g$ | overlap | $< 2\ \text{eV}$ | $> 4\ \text{eV}$ |
| Conductivity | $\sim 10^7\ \text{S/m}$ | between | $\sim 10^{-13}\ \text{S/m}$ |
| Bonding | metallic | covalent | ionic |
| Examples | Cu, Hg, Al, Ag | Ge, Si | wood, rubber, mica |
