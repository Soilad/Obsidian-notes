# $n_{0} = N_{c}\exp{(-\frac{E_{C} - E_{F}}{kT})}$
- $N_{c}$: Effective density in conduction band
- $E_{C}-E_{F}$: Fermi energy with respect to conduction band
# $p_{0} = N_{v}\exp{(-\frac{E_{F} - E_{V}}{kT})}$
- $N_{v}$: Effective density in valence band
- $E_{F}-E_{V}$: Fermi energy with respect to valence band
