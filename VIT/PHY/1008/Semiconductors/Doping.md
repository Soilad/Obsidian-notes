> Adding impurity atoms to a pure single crystal to raise conductivity.

- Use Group 3 (trivalent) or Group 5 (pentavalent) elements.
- Conductivity of semiconductor controlled by doping.

## N-type (pentavalent donor)
- Examples: P, As, Sb. Five valence electrons.
- Extra electron forms a **donor level** just below CB.
- Electrons are majority carriers.
- Fermi level moves up, toward CB.

## P-type (trivalent acceptor)
- Examples: B, Ga, In, Al. Three valence electrons.
- Missing bond creates a **hole**. Holes are majority carriers.
- Fermi level moves down, toward VB.

## Intrinsic Silicon
- Si: atomic number 14, 4 valence electrons.
- At 0 K: insulator.
- At room temperature: few electron-hole pairs from broken covalent bonds.
