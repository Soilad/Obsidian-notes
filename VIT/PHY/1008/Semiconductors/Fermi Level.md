> Highest occupied electron energy level at absolute zero.

- At 0 K, electrons fill levels up to the Fermi level.
- Fermi energy = Fermi level at 0 K. Also max kinetic energy of electron at 0 K.
- Fermi energy is constant for each solid.
- Intrinsic semiconductor: Fermi level sits at mid-gap, halfway between VB and CB.
- Conductor: Fermi level inside a band (CB partly filled).
