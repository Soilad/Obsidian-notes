> Is the redistribution of intensity produced when two or more **coherent light waves** superpose.
### For two waves:
$$
\begin{align}
y_1​=a_1​cosωt\\
y_2​=a_2​cos(ωt+ϕ)
\end{align}
$$
- the resultant intensity is
$$
\begin{align}
I=I_1​+I_2​+2I_1​I_2​​cosϕ​
\end{align}
$$
- where ϕ is the phase difference.
## Interference Intensity Distribution
$$
\begin{align}
I_1 = I_2 = I_0\\
\implies I = 2I_0(1+\cos\phi)\\
\implies I = 4I_0\cos^2\frac{\phi}{2}\\
\end{align}
$$
## Maximum intensity
$$
\begin{align}
ϕ=2mπ\\
I_{max}​=(\sqrt I_1​​+\sqrt I_2​​)^2​
\end{align}
$$
- For equal intensities  $I_1​=I_2​=I_0​$
	> $I_{max}​=4I_0​​$

## Minimum intensity
$$
\begin{align}
ϕ=(2m+1)π\\
I_{min}​=(I_1​​−I_2​^​)2​
\end{align}
$$
- For equal intensities,
	> $I_{min}​=0​$
