> Magnetic moment per unit volume. Shows how strongly a material magnetizes. Unit: A/m.

## $M = \frac{\text{Magnetic moment}}{\text{Volume}}$
## $M = \chi H$
## $B = \mu_0(H + M)$

- $\chi$ = [[Magnetic Susceptibility]] (dimensionless)
- Physical meaning: the material's response, how atomic moments align. See [[Magnetic Moment]].

## Example
$H = 156\ \text{A/m}$, $\chi = 1.2\times10^{-4}$
## $M = (1.2\times10^{-4})(156) = 0.01872\ \text{A/m}$
## $B \approx 1.96\times10^{-4}\ \text{T}$
