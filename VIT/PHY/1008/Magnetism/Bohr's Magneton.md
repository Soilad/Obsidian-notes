> Natural unit of magnetic moment
## $\mu_{B}=\frac{e\hbar}{2m_{e}}$
## From Magnetic Moment
$$
\begin{align}
\mu &=I\cdot A\\
I &= \frac{e}{T}\\
A &= \pi r^2\\
T &= \frac{2\pi r}{v}\\
\implies I &= \frac{ev}{2\pi r}\\
\implies \mu &= \frac{ev}{2\pi r}\cdot\pi r^2\\
\implies \mu &= \frac{evr}{2}\\
L &= mvr\\
\implies \mu &= \frac{e}{2m_{e}}L\\
\end{align}
$$
## From Bohr's Postulate
$$
\begin{align}
L &= n\hbar\\
\implies \mu &= \frac{e}{2m_{e}}n\hbar\\
\end{align}
$$
