> Diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic. Sorted by $\chi$, $\mu_r$, and M-H shape.

| Type | $\chi$ | $\mu_r$ | M-H curve | $\chi$ vs T |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diamagnetic | $< 0$ | $< 1$ | straight, negative slope | independent |
| Paramagnetic | $> 0$ small | $> 1$ | straight, positive slope | $\chi = C/T$ |
| Ferromagnetic | $\gg 0$ | $\gg 1$ | hysteresis loop | phase transition at Curie T |

- Ferromagnets keep memory. Paramagnets and superparamagnets do not.
- See [[Magnetic Susceptibility]], [[Hysteresis Loop]], [[Superparamagnetism]].
