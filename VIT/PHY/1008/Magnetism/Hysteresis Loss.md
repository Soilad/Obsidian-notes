> Energy dissipated as heat per cycle of magnetization. Equal to area of the B-H loop.

## $dW = H\,dB$ (work per unit volume)
## $W_{loss} = \oint H\,dB$ (per unit volume per cycle)

- Larger loop area means higher loss.
- Soft magnets have small loss. Use in transformers.
- See [[Hysteresis Loop]]. Loss in transformer cores: [[Transformers]].
