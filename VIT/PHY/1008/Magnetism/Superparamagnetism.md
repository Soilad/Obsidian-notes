> Strong, reversible magnetism in nanoparticles of ferromagnetic material.

- Occurs in nanoparticles of ferromagnetic materials.
- Strong response, nanoparticle scale. Paramagnetism is weak and atomic scale.
- No memory effect: no hysteresis. Magnetism switches on and off quickly.
- Use: medical imaging (MRI contrast agents).
