> Number of magnetic field lines passing perpendicular through unit area. Also called magnetic induction.

## $\Phi = \vec{B}\cdot\vec{A}$
## $B = \frac{\Phi}{A}$
- Units: $\text{Wb/m}^2$ or Tesla ($\text{T}$)
- Lines of force are imaginary. Magnetic monopole does not exist in practice.
- Physical meaning: $B$ is the actual field inside a material. It includes the external applied field and the material's response. See [[Magnetic Field Strength]] and [[Magnetization]]. Basic flux concept: [[Magnetic Flux]].

## Max flux
## $\Phi_{max} = B\cdot A$ (when $\cos\theta = 1$)

## Angle between $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$
## $\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{B}\cdot\vec{A}}{|\vec{B}|\,|\vec{A}|}$
