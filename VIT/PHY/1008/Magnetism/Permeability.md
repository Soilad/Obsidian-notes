> Ability of a material to support a magnetic field. Ratio of $B$ to $H$.

## $B = \mu H$
## $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\ \text{H/m}$ (free space)
## $\mu_r = \frac{\mu}{\mu_0}$
## $\mu = \mu_0(1+\chi)$

## Classification by $\mu_r$
- $\mu_r > 1$: paramagnetic or ferromagnetic
- $\mu_r < 1$: diamagnetic
- $\mu_r \approx 1$: non-magnetic

## Example
Solenoid field $8\times10^{-4}\ \text{T}$ in air. With iron core $2.2\ \text{T}$.
## $\mu_r = \frac{2.2}{8\times10^{-4}} = 2750$

Wave speed in a medium uses $\mu$: [[Electromagnetic Waves]].
