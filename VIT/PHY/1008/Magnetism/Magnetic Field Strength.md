> External magnetizing force. The "cause" of magnetization. Unit: A/m.

## $B = \mu H$
## $H = \frac{NI}{L}$ (inside a long solenoid, see [[Toroid and Solenoid]])

- $H$ comes from the external source, usually coil or magnet current.
- $B$ comes from $H$ plus the material's response. See [[Magnetic Flux Density]].
- $\mu$ is permeability. See [[Permeability]].
