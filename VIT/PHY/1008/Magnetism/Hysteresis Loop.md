> Plot of $M$ vs $H$ (or $B$ vs $H$) for a ferromagnet. Shows memory of past field.

## Key points on the M-H loop
- **Saturation**: all moments aligned. $M$ stops rising.
- **Remanence**: $M$ left when $H$ returns to zero.
- **Coercivity**: reverse $H$ needed to bring $M$ to zero.

## Key points on the B-H loop
- Saturation (a and d)
- Retentivity (b): $B$ at $H = 0$
- Coercivity (c): $H$ where $B = 0$

## Soft vs hard
- Soft magnetic: narrow loop. Used in transformer cores.
- Hard magnetic: wide loop. Used in permanent magnets.

Above the Curie temperature the loop becomes a straight line. See [[Curie's Law]].

Loop area = energy lost per cycle. See [[Hysteresis Loss]]. Soft cores in devices: [[Transformers]].
