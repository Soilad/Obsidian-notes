> Dimensionless measure of how easily a material magnetizes in an external field.

## $\chi = \frac{M}{H}$
## $\mu_r = 1 + \chi$

## Sign meaning
- $\chi > 0$: paramagnetic or ferromagnetic
- $\chi < 0$: diamagnetic
- $\chi \approx 0$: non-magnetic

## Temperature dependence
- Diamagnetic: negative, nearly independent of temperature
- Paramagnetic: follows [[Curie's Law]], $\chi = \frac{C}{T}$
- Ferromagnetic: phase transition at the Curie temperature

## Example
$\mu_r = 13$ when $\chi = 12$. Large $\mu_r$ means ferromagnetic.
Given $M = 3000\ \text{A/m}$, $H = 250\ \text{A/m}$: $\chi = \frac{3000}{250} = 12$.

See [[Magnetization]] and [[Permeability]].
