> Magnetic dipole strength of a current loop.

## $\mu = N\cdot I\cdot A$
- $N$ = number of turns
- $I$ = current
- $A$ = loop area

A current loop acts like a bar magnet. See [[Magnetic Dipole]] and [[Moving charges and Magnetism]]. See [[Bohr's Magneton]] for the electron orbital case.

## Electron orbit
## $\mu = \frac{e}{2m_e}L$
## $\mu_n = \frac{e}{2m_e} n\hbar$

Not to be confused with permeability (see [[Permeability]]).
