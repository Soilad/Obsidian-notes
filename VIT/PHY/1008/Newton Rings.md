Concentric bright and dark circular fringes made by interference reflected from upper and lower surcaces of thin air film between:
- a plano-convex lens
- a plane glass plate
## Thickness
$$
\begin{align}
(R-t)^2+r^2 = R^2\\
\implies R^2+t^2-2Rt+r^2 = R^2\\
\implies r^2 \approx 2Rt\\
\implies t \approx \frac{r^2}{2R}\\
\end{align}
$$
## Diameter of Dark Newton Ring ([[Thin Film]])
$$
\begin{align}
2t = n\lambda\\
t = \frac{r_n^2}{2R}\\
\implies \frac{r_n^2}{R} = n\lambda\\
\implies r_n^2 = n\lambda R\\
\implies D_n^2 = 4n\lambda R\\
\end{align}
$$
## Diameter of Bright Newton Ring ([[Thin Film]])
$$
\begin{align}
2t = (n+\frac{1}{2})\lambda\\
\implies r_n^2 = (n+\frac{1}{2})\lambda R\\
\implies D_n^2 = 4(n+\frac{1}{2})\lambda R
\end{align}
$$
### Wavelength From Newton Rings
$$
\begin{align}
D_n^2 = 4n\lambda R\\
D_{n+p}^2 = 4(n+p)\lambda R\\
\implies D_{n+p}^2 - D_n^2 = 4p\lambda R\\
\implies \lambda = \frac{D_{n+p}^2 - D_n^2}{4pR}\\
\end{align}
$$
### Radius of Curvature From Newton Rings
$$
\begin{align}
D_n^2 = 4n\lambda R\\
\implies R = \frac{D_n^2}{4n\lambda}\\
\implies R = \frac{D_{n+p}^2 - D_n^2}{4p\lambda}\\
\end{align}
$$
